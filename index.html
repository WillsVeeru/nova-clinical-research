A physician-managed research site built on science and compassion

Based in DeSoto, Texas, NOVA Clinical Research – U.S. partners with pharmaceutical sponsors and CROs to conduct carefully managed clinical studies. Our board-certified investigators, multilingual staff, and dedicated research facility serve the diverse communities of North Texas.
