Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Information about how we handle website information and the terms of use for this website.

Information collected through this website

Our contact forms ask only for your name, email address, optional phone number, organization (for sponsor and business inquiries), the study you are interested in, and your message. When you submit a form, your device's email application opens a pre-filled message addressed to our research office; the website itself does not store form submissions. Please do not include detailed medical information.

How we use your information

Information you send is used only to respond to your inquiry and is routed to authorized NOVA research personnel. We do not sell your information. Health information collected during a clinical study is handled as described in that study's informed consent document and applicable privacy laws.

How to contact the research team

Visitors may also call our office at (469) 505-3451 or email research@novacr.us. Information shared by phone or email is handled by the research team according to its policies and procedures.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or analytics services to understand general usage patterns and improve user experience.

Third-party services

We may use service providers for website hosting and related infrastructure services. These providers process information on our behalf in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Terms of use

Content on this website is provided for general informational purposes. Study listings and site information may change without notice.

Accessibility

We aim to keep this website usable with a keyboard and screen reader. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact us so we can address it.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Dr. Umer Hafeez, Director of Clinical Research and Development, at (469) 505-3451.
