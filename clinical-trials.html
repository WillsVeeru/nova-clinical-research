How Participation Works

From first inquiry to
follow-up

Every study is different, but the path generally follows these stages. Participation is always voluntary.

  1. Step 01

    Find a Study

    Review the studies currently listed by our research team.

  2. Step 02

    Contact Our Team

    Reach out by phone or inquiry form to ask questions and learn about next steps.

  3. Step 03

    Pre-Screening

    The research team reviews whether you may meet basic study requirements.

  4. Step 04

    Informed Consent

    The study is explained in detail and you decide — without pressure — whether to take part.

  5. Step 05

    Study Participation

    Eligible participants follow the visit schedule described in the study protocol.

  6. Step 06

    Follow-Up

    The research team completes required follow-up activities and remains available for questions.
