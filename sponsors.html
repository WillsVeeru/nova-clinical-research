Capabilities

Research infrastructure & operations

Verified site capabilities supporting protocol execution and data quality.

Clinical research infrastructure

  • 4 exam rooms dedicated to research
  • Complete PFTs and DLCO on site
  • EKG
  • Facilities for extended-stay pharmacokinetic studies
  • CLIA-waived diagnostic laboratory
  • Point-of-care testing: urinalysis, strep, flu, COVID-19, urine drug screen, pregnancy

Investigational product management

  • Secured investigational drug storage
  • Double-lock refrigerators; −20 °C and −70 °C freezers
  • 24/7 temperature recording with backup generator
  • Laminar flow hood and sterile drug preparation area
  • Dedicated pharmacy specialist
  • Regular and refrigerated centrifuge

Research technology

  • CTMS with eSource, eReg, eConsent, and eISF
  • Remote access for CRAs

Quality & protocol compliance

  • Annually calibrated medical equipment
  • Institutional Biosafety Committee (IBC) approval
  • Certified raters for dermatology, neurology, and psychiatry studies
Recruitment & Retention

Diverse communities, multilingual care

Our site serves the rapidly growing, diverse communities of southern Dallas County. A multilingual staff — English, Spanish, Punjabi, Urdu, Hindi, Vietnamese, and various African languages — supports clear communication and participant retention.
