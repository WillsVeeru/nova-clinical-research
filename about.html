NOVA Clinical Research – U.S. partners with pharmaceutical sponsors, contract research organizations, and investigators to conduct clinical studies according to their written protocols. Our investigators are board-certified specialists who bring daily clinical experience to every study they oversee.

Being physician-managed means responsive communication, operational agility, and deep connections to the communities we serve — including a multilingual staff that helps participants feel understood from the first conversation.

Our Mission

To support high-quality clinical research while creating a professional, respectful, and participant-focused research experience.

Our Vision

To be a trusted clinical research partner in North Texas — discovering, innovating, and helping transform lives through ethical science.

Core Values

Four principles guide our work

Participant Respect

Clear communication, informed consent, and a respectful experience at every visit.

Research Quality

Careful protocol execution and attention to accurate, complete study data.

Scientific Integrity

Honest reporting and adherence to the written study plan and applicable requirements.

Collaborative Partnership

Responsive communication with sponsors, CROs, investigators, and referring physicians.

Ethical Research

Our commitment to ethical clinical research

Participant safety, rights, and well-being come before every other consideration.

  1. 01

    Every study is reviewed and approved by an Institutional Review Board (IRB) before enrollment begins.

  2. 02

    Informed consent is an ongoing conversation — participants may ask questions and withdraw at any time.

  3. 03

    Studies are conducted according to the protocol and applicable Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines.

  4. 04

    Participant privacy and the confidentiality of health information are protected throughout the study.
